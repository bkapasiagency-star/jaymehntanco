About Us

Meet CA Jay Mehta

FCA
CMA
DISA
B.Com
ICAI Member
Surat, Gujarat

Jay Mehta is a qualified Chartered Accountant and a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) since 2017, with expertise in ITR Filing, Audit & Assurance, Taxation Compliance, GST Compliance, and Corporate Advisory Services.

He has extensive experience working with startups, SMEs, corporates, and overseas businesses, helping them manage taxation, regulatory compliance, financial reporting, and business operations efficiently in India.

His core areas of expertise include Income Tax Return Filing, Statutory Audit, Internal Audit, GST Advisory, ROC Compliance, Corporate Tax Planning, and Business Consulting.

Known for his practical approach and strong understanding of Indian tax laws and corporate regulations, Jay focuses on delivering accurate, timely, and business-oriented financial solutions that help clients stay compliant and grow confidently.
